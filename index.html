How We Work

Built on a Print Bed

Every product is designed in FreeCAD and sliced in OrcaSlicer — no middleman, no markup, no compromise.

🖨️
FDM Printing

PLA+, PETG, and TPU filaments. Layer resolution down to 0.1mm for functional and decorative parts alike.

📐
Custom Design

Send us your idea — we model it in FreeCAD from scratch. Tolerances held to ±0.2mm on all functional fits.

📦
Fast Fulfilment

Most orders ship within 48 hours. Local Surrey delivery available for orders over 0.

♻️
Responsible Materials

PLA+ is plant-based and compostable. Offcuts and failed prints are recycled — nothing hits the landfill.
